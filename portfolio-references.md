**[whatareyouuptoday.com](https://whatareyouuptoday.com/)**
- I love the messaging via copy
- Love how well yellow works as an accent on an otherwise basic website design
- Love the project categorisation
- Hero page looks clean + main writeup explains the agency/individual well
- Does a good job with the flow of how it just flows into the case studies below

**[failennaselta.com](https://www.failennaselta.com/)**
- Want to copy this portfolio but also don't, because that won't be right
- Love everything about it — it's minimal, love the otherwise monotone illustrations and pops of color
- Love the flow of how the case studies pop out after scroll — simple interaction design but it works
- Love the extra stuff in folders on a y2k grey 2000s computer screen — can use this for my project buckets outside product design
- I like how the project images are comfortably spaced with inviting images and are not overwhelming to look at
- Case study buddy: love how the background is clean enough for the content to stand out without too much effort
- Case study accent stands out with ease; the typeface width is easy on the eyes, so spacing out text properly is important, especially individual letters
- Case study: AI rounded-corners text card, but looks good
- Case study: section title on the left is muted when not selected, black when on the section — easy on the eyes and serves its purpose without trying to be fancy

**[emilybackes.design](https://emilybackes.design/)**
- Loooove the header page and interaction design

- Love the hero page visual + font choice + general feel — could use as inspo

- Great resume reference

**[ayumilee.com/auto-earn](https://www.ayumilee.com/auto-earn)**
